About credit approval

A customer can only buy on "Pay Later" terms once an administrator ticks credit approved.

Their credit limit is then set on the borrower profile. A sale that would push them past that limit is refused at the point of sale, before any stock moves.

Managers can register customers but cannot grant credit approval.

About the ID photo

Both pictures are optional. Nobody is turned away for not carrying a card.

Only staff who may edit customers can open the ID photo. Everybody else sees that one is on file and no more.
